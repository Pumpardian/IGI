This Privacy Policy describes the procedure for processing and using personal data collected by the animal store "Pet Shop". We care about the confidentiality of your data and take all necessary measures to protect it.

1. Information Collection

We collect personal information that you provide to us directly, for example, when registering on the website, submitting an application, or subscribing to a newsletter. This may include your name, email address, phone number, and other contact details.

2. Use of Information

We use the collected data to provide the services you request, improve the quality of service, communicate with you regarding your requests, and provide information about new offers and promotions.

3. Data Disclosure to Third Parties

We do not disclose your personal data to third parties, except as provided by law or when necessary for the provision of our services (for example, transferring data to partners to complete a transaction).

4. Data Security

We take necessary measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Access to personal data is limited only to those employees who need it to perform their job duties.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, and restrict its processing. You can also withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.

6. Policy Changes

We reserve the right to change this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated version on our website.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or comments regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email: info@petshop.com.
